Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript "Do Fine-Tuned Adapters Survive Foundation-Model Upgrades? A Decision Model and Evidence from Open Model Families" for consideration in the INFORMS Journal on Data Science.

Many organizations now build their AI capabilities by attaching small fine-tuned adapters to foundation models licensed from a provider. Every new model release forces a decision that most teams make by habit: retrain the adapter, keep the old one, or try a cheaper repair. The manuscript treats this as a data-driven decision problem. An optimal stopping model gives a closed-form retraining threshold and a price ceiling for any repair method, both driven by a quantity a team can measure, the share of the retraining benefit that the cheaper option keeps. We measure that quantity on 22 upgrades of open models across three tasks, and we estimate the release rate, the model's main economic input, from the release histories of six model families.

The data analysis produced several findings that we think will interest the journal's readers. Routine continued pre-training updates left most of an adapter intact, so retraining paid off only for very valuable adapters. Moving from a base model to an instruction-tuned one often destroyed the adapter, and tracing one model family through its published post-training stages shows that supervised fine-tuning, not the later preference or reinforcement-learning updates, is the step that does the damage. A label-free measure of activation drift predicted recovery well within a model family but did not transfer across families, and we document where it fails. Release intervals turned out to be more regular than the Poisson baseline, and the decision rule is robust to that difference.

We believe the paper fits the journal's aim of publishing data science work that informs decisions in business and industry. All code, configurations, and result files are publicly available, and every experiment can be reproduced on a single laptop GPU.

This manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. The author has no competing interests to declare. The use of generative AI assistance in preparing the work is disclosed in the manuscript.

Thank you for considering this submission.
