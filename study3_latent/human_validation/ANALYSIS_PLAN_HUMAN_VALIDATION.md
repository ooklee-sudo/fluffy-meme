# Plan: human validation of the latent-class (gold-standard-free) estimates in study 3

Written 2026-10-16, before any human label is collected. Already seen: all classifier outputs and the latent-class results
(results_latent.json), and the Hormuz validation, in which the latent-class estimates did not match the human labels.

## Sample and coding
- 200 reviews, 50 per DiD cell, drawn at random (seed 20261016) from the 1,200 reviews coded by all four classifiers
  (human_val/make_validation_sheets.py; key in validation_key_PRIVATE.csv, not shown to coders).
- Two coders, working alone from the text only, each with a different random order, code three fields with the study codebook:
  overall (neg/neu/pos), defect (0/1), performance (0/-1/1/2). Outcomes: defect = 1; negative = overall neg; performance praised = 1.
- Disagreements on an outcome are resolved by an independent third coder who sees only the text of the disputed reviews
  (sheet generated by analysis_human_validation.py); the final label is the majority of the three. The investigators do not adjudicate.

## Comparisons (per outcome)
1. Human-coder agreement (Cohen's kappa, coder 1 vs coder 2).
2. Human-based sensitivity, specificity and Youden index J of each classifier (LLM H, S, Z, L) against the final human labels,
   weighted by cell (each cell's 50 reviews weighted by its share of the 17,476 reviews), with bootstrap 95% intervals.
3. Agreement with the latent-class estimates (results_latent.json): whether the latent estimate of the LLM's J lies inside the
   human-based 95% interval and the absolute difference in J.
4. Human-based cell prevalences and DiD versus the latent-class cell prevalences and DiD.

## Decision rules (fixed now)
- For an outcome, the latent-class correction is judged validated if the latent J of the LLM lies inside the human-based 95%
  interval and differs from the human-based J by at most 0.10.
- If validated for an outcome, the paper reports the corrected estimate as primary for it; otherwise the paper reports that the
  gold-standard-free correction could not be validated for that outcome and relies on the human-based J (corrected DiD = FE DiD / human J).
- All differences are reported whatever the decision.
