# "White goods" (major household appliances) after the March 2026 war onset (exploratory; scripts `analysis_whitegoods.py` and an inline FRED analysis; outputs `results_whitegoods*.json`)
Same rolling-origin method as the MRTS plan (36-month month-effects + trend forecast; ten reference years).
- **Appliance retail (Census MRTS, NSA, nominal).** Household appliance stores (443141, small category): Mar–Jul +2.1% vs trend (perm p = 0.60), July +6.3%; 24-month training −0.2%, 48-month +6.3%, no trend +7.0%. Electronics and appliance stores (443): +5.6% (p = 0.20). Furniture and home furnishings (442): +1.0%. None shows a decline.
- **PCE durable goods (FRED PCEDG, nominal SA, through Aug 2026):** +2.3% (Mar–Jul) / +2.6% (Mar–Aug) above trend (p = 0.2; reference range −3.1% to +1.8%).
- **Production of major household appliances (FRED IPG33522S, NAICS 33522, index SA, Jan–Aug 2026: 88.9, 86.5, 87.0, 88.5, 90.9, 91.2, 91.7, 92.5):** −3.7% vs trend over Mar–Aug (two-sided perm p = 0.5; low-tail p = 0.2; reference range −6.5% to +7.3%); 24-month training −2.1%, 48-month −4.8%, no trend −5.3% (low p = 0.1). Production fell in February–March but has recovered each month since March. Household appliances overall (IPG3352S) −3.8% (p = 0.4); small electrical appliances (IPG33521S) −6.2% (p = 0.7).
**Reading.** Retail sales of appliances were not below trend (nominal, tariff-affected); factory output of major appliances is about 4% below trend through August 2026 but within past variation and recovering. No significant decline in white goods sales.
