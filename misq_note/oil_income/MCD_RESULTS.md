# McDonald's alone, US comparable sales (exploratory; plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN_MCD.md`, script `analysis_mcd.py`, output `results_mcd.json`)
McDonald's US comps (%): 2025Q1 −3.6, 2025Q2 +2.5, 2025Q3 +2.4, 2025Q4 +6.8, 2026Q1 +3.9, 2026Q2 +0.8.
- **M1 (own history).** Δ_2026Q2 (Q2 minus Q1) = −3.1 pp, 3rd lowest of 16 quarterly changes (2022Q2–2025Q4 plus 2026Q2); one-sided rank p = 0.19. Not unusual for McDonald's: changes of −3.2, −3.8 and −2.3 pp occurred in 2023–2024. Δ at the 2022 gasoline peak (2022Q2) = +0.2 pp (9th lowest of 12), no decline.
- **M2 (versus the other 12 chains).** McDonald's Δ_2026Q2 minus the peers' mean Δ (+0.92 pp) = −4.0 pp; 2nd lowest of 12 (reference 2023Q2–2025Q4), p = 0.17; z = −1.7 against the peer cross-section (SD 2.3 pp). Suggestive, not significant. In 2022Q2 McDonald's was above its peers (+2.1 pp).
- **M3 (year-ago / base effects).** The fall from +3.9% to +0.8% mirrors the base: 2025Q1 was −3.6% (easy comparison) and 2025Q2 +2.5% (harder). The two-year stack (current + year-ago growth) **rose** from +0.3 (2026Q1) to +3.3 (2026Q2) (+3.0 pp; peers on average −0.4 pp). Year-over-year 2026Q2 comps are 1.7 pp lower than 2025Q2's comps (0.8 vs 2.5), but 2026Q1 was 7.5 pp above 2025Q1.
**Reading.** McDonald's growth slowed in 2026Q2 and was weaker than peers, but the slowdown is mostly a base effect (stack improved), is within McDonald's own historical range, and the statistical evidence is weak (ranks 2–3 of 12–16). It does not demonstrate a shock-induced collapse. Management (see `MCD_INCOME_EVIDENCE.md`) attributes about two thirds of the traffic shortfall to value-menu execution. The premise that McDonald's is the lowest-priced chain was not tested here.
