# Korean stock market reaction to the 2026 Iran war and Trump's statements (the market that reacts one day after the U.S.): analysis plan

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14 at the author's question. Only file date coverage was inspected; no Korean return was examined.
**Data.** Yahoo Finance (chart API), daily closes in local dates, 2 Jan 2025–14 Oct 2026: KOSPI (^KS11), KOSDAQ (^KQ11), Samsung Electronics (005930.KS), SK hynix (000660.KS), USD/KRW (KRW=X), S&P 500 (^GSPC), Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC). U.S. data as in `../bigtech/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`.
**Timing.** The Korean session (about 00:00–06:30 UTC) closes before the U.S. session opens, so the U.S. close of day d (ET) is reflected in the Korean close of the next Korean trading date after d. For each Korean date t, "previous U.S. session" = the latest U.S. trading date strictly before t (date-wise; the Korean date t is ahead of ET).
**Part A (transmission).** r_KOSPI,t = a + b·r_S&P,prev(t) + e_t, OLS with HAC (5 lags), over (i) 2025-01-02 to 2026-02-27 (pre-war) and (ii) 2026-03-02 to 2026-10-14 (war); test whether b and R² differ (Chow test of b); the same for KOSDAQ, Samsung, SK hynix.
**Part B (events).** The 12 Trump-statement events from `../bigtech/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`. Korean reaction day = the first Korean trading date after the U.S. event date (reaction to a statement made during U.S. hours); also Korean date equal to the U.S. event date (statement made earlier, i.e., during U.S. night/Korean daytime; reported as a second window). Abnormal return AR = r_KOSPI,t − (a + b r_S&P,prev(t)) with (a, b) estimated on the pre-war window; KOSPI also raw. Tests (as in the U.S. study): mean |AR| on event reaction days versus random Korean trading days since 2 March (permutation, 5,000 draws, event days excluded); mean signed AR for threats (expected negative) and call-offs/deals (expected positive).
**Part C (level).** Mean |daily return| for KOSPI, Samsung and SK hynix before vs. since the war; the KOSPI cumulative return from 27 Feb to 13 Oct; USD/KRW change.
**Limitations.** Korea's market reacts to many Korea-specific events (BOK, chips, policy); the 'previous U.S. session' model removes only the U.S. spillover; unknown statement times; 12 events; Samsung and SK hynix are chip-heavy and correlate with Nvidia/Broadcom.
