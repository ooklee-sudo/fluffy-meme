# Korean stock market and the Iran war / Trump statements (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`; `analysis_korea.py`; `results_korea.json`)
Yahoo Finance closes (local dates), 2 Jan 2025–14 Oct 2026. A return-computation bug (union-calendar differences dropped returns after one-market holidays) was found and fixed before these results; an earlier run with the bug gave similar conclusions.
**A. U.S.→Korea transmission (KOSPI on the previous U.S. session's S&P 500 return).** Pre-war (Jan 2025–Feb 2026): β = 0.58 (R² 0.20, n = 278). Since 2 March 2026: β = **1.77** (R² 0.13, n = 152); difference p = 0.001. KOSDAQ 0.49 → 1.44 (p = 0.013); Samsung Electronics 0.67 → 1.97 (p = 0.018); SK hynix 1.18 → 2.20 (p = 0.083). Korean stocks became about three times as sensitive to the overnight U.S. move, and the share of variance explained fell (volatility from other sources rose).
**B. Trump-statement events (12; Korean reaction day = first Korean trading day after the U.S. date).** Mean |abnormal KOSPI return| (vs the U.S.-spillover model) = 3.31% on reaction days vs 2.81% on other days since 2 March (permutation p = 0.25); threats: mean AR −0.99% (p = 0.29 for 'negative'); call-offs/deals: +0.80% (p = 0.29 for 'positive'). Directions as hypothesized, none significant. Same-calendar-date window: 2.23% (p = 0.74). Largest reactions (raw KOSPI): 9 Mar −6.15% after the 6 Mar 'unconditional surrender' statement (Samsung −8.1%, SK hynix −10.0%); 8 Apr +6.65% after the 7 Apr 'civilization will die' statement (coincident with the two-week ceasefire announced 7–8 April); 12 Jun +4.53% after the 11 Jun strike cancellation; **23 Jun −10.53%** after the 22 Jun 'hit Iran very hard again' (Samsung −13.1%, SK hynix −13.3%); 18 Jun +2.2% after the memorandum signing; 12 Oct −3.7% after the 8 Oct call-off. The 23 March call-off day itself saw KOSPI −6.7% on the Korean date (before the U.S. reaction).
**C. Levels and volatility.** Mean |daily KOSPI return|: 1.1% before the war, 3.0% since (Samsung 1.8% → 4.2%, SK hynix 2.7% → 4.9%; USD/KRW 0.45% → 0.56%). KOSPI fell 7.5% on 3 March and 12.9% on 4 March (the first Korean sessions after the weekend and the 2 March holiday), rebounded +9.2% on 5 March, and touched −19% from its 27 Feb close (6,244) on 31 March (5,052); by 14 Oct it is at 6,131 (−1.8%). Samsung +9.9%, SK hynix +38.7%, won stronger (USD/KRW −6.6%). 33 days with |return| > 5% since 2 March, including +16.5% on 31 July and −11.5% on 28 July.
**Reading.** The Korean market reacted extremely to the war onset (−19% peak to trough in March) and became much more sensitive to U.S. overnight moves; on Trump-statement days the Korean reaction is large in several cases (23 June, 9 March, 8 April) but not significantly different from the market's very high ordinary volatility (p = 0.25 for the magnitude test, 12 events). The next-day timing matters: the Korean reaction to a statement made during U.S. hours appears on the next Korean trading date. Semiconductor stocks (Samsung, SK hynix) drive much of it.
