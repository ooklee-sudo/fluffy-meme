**Status.** Written 2026-10-14 at the author's request. Disclosure: the KOSPI path and reactions on selected days (3–5 March, 22–23 June) were already examined (`../korea/RESULTS.md`); no airline series has been examined.
**Data.** Yahoo Finance daily closes (local dates), 2 Jan 2025–14 Oct 2026: Korean Air Lines (003490.KS), Jeju Air (089590.KS), Jin Air (272450.KS), T'way Air (091810.KS), United (UAL), Delta (DAL), American (AAL), U.S. Global Jets ETF (JETS), KOSPI, S&P 500; WTI (FRED DCOILWTICO, through 6 Oct). Asiana is no longer listed separately (merged into Korean Air).
**Part 1: onset.** CAR over the first 5 and 20 Korean trading days from 3 March (2 March holiday) for the Korean airlines (market model on the previous U.S. session S&P 500 return, estimated 3 Mar 2025–30 Jan 2026); U.S. airlines from 2 March (market model on the S&P 500). Empirical p from rolling windows of the estimation period. Hypothesis (directional): airlines fell (jet fuel cost, airspace) — CAR[0,4] < 0.
**Part 2: path.** Cumulative return from the 27 Feb close to 13/14 Oct, maximum drawdown after 2 March and its date, the peak and its date, versus the broad index.
**Part 3: oil sensitivity.** r_t = a + b·Δln WTI_prev + c·r_S&P,prev + e (HAC 5 lags), pre-war (to 27 Feb 2026) and war (2 Mar–6 Oct 2026) periods, interaction test; Korean Air vs U.S. airlines. Hypothesis: b < 0, larger in absolute value during the war.
**Part 4: Trump-statement events.** The 12 events from `../bigtech/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`: abnormal return of Korean Air on the next Korean trading date (and of UAL/DAL on days 0–1), magnitude test against random days since 2 March (permutation, 5,000), signed tests (threats negative, call-offs/deals positive).
**Limitations.** Airline stocks also respond to travel demand, currency (USD/KRW affects Korean airlines' costs and revenues), cargo business (Korean Air's cargo and defense/aerospace divisions), and merger-related news; 12 news-selected events; closing prices only.
