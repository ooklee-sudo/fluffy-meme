# OPEC+ announcement days as oil-supply shocks unrelated to Trump's Iran statements (plan fixed before computing; written 2026-10-14)

**Why.** The author pointed out that, in a regression of chip (or airline) returns on oil prices, oil is the problematic regressor because it moves with war news. OPEC+ output announcements are a more exogenous source of oil-price variation, though during a Hormuz disruption the group's ability to change physical supply is limited.
**Dates (from the OPEC and press releases found by search; the list is incomplete: March, April and July meetings were not found).** Seven-country meetings on Sunday 3 May (increase of 188 kb/d for June), Sunday 7 June (scheduled; decision not verified), Sunday 2 August (188 kb/d increase for September), Sunday 6 September (September targets kept for October), Sunday 4 October (November targets kept). Reaction day = the next trading day in each market (Monday 4 May, 8 June, 3 August, Tuesday 8 September because Monday 7 September is Labor Day, Monday 5 October; in Korea the next Korean trading date after the U.S. date).
**Tests.** (1) First stage: |daily WTI change| on the reaction days vs. other days since 2 March (permutation, 5,000). (2) Reduced form: Korean Air, SK hynix, KOSPI and United returns on those days. No significance claims are expected with five dates; the purpose is to see whether the instrument moves oil at all.
**Limitations.** Five announcements, incomplete list, the decisions were largely expected (anticipated by the market), the war dominates oil prices.
