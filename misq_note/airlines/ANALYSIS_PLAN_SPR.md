# Strategic-reserve (IEA/SPR) announcement days as oil-supply policy shocks: plan (fixed before computing; written 2026-10-14)

**Dates found by search (not by looking at prices).** IEA extraordinary Governing Board 10 March; IEA announcement of the 400 million-barrel coordinated release 11 March 2026 (Korea's 22.46 million barrels announced 11–12 March); U.S. DOE strategic petroleum reserve exchange announcements/solicitations: 9 April (up to 30 million barrels), 30 April (up to 92.5 million), 10 June (40 million), 29 September (40 million); IEA statement on accelerating the release, 9 October. The list is incomplete (the U.S. release was announced around 11 March but its exact date was not found; other IEA/national actions may exist). These are supply-policy responses to the war, so they are not independent of it: 10 June coincides with Trump's 'very hard tonight' post, 9 April follows the 7–8 April ceasefire. They are used as a less market-driven source of oil variation, with that caveat.
**Reaction day.** U.S. date d (WTI, U.S. stocks, d and d+1); Korea: the next Korean trading date after d.
**Tests.** (1) First stage: |WTI change| on the reaction days vs. other days since 2 March (permutation 5,000) and the signed changes. (2) Reduced form: Korean Air, Jin Air, SK hynix, KOSPI and United on those days; the sign of Korean Air's return relative to the WTI change. (3) Using these days as the 'oil shock' sample, the ratio ΔKorean Air / ΔWTI (Wald estimator) if the first stage is meaningful.
**Limitations.** Six dates; the releases were widely anticipated; they coincide with war events; the instrument may be weak. The result is descriptive.
