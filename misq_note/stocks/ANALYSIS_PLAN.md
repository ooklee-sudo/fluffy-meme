# Stock-market reaction of U.S. industries to the 2026 Iran war: event-study plan (fixed before any 2026 return is examined)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14. Only file headers, date coverage and industry names were inspected; no 2026 return was looked at.
**Data.** Kenneth French Data Library, daily returns (percent) of 48 value-weighted U.S. industry portfolios and the daily Fama-French factors (Mkt-RF, SMB, HML, RF), through 31 Aug 2026.
**Event.** War onset Saturday 28 Feb 2026; first trading day after = Monday 2 March 2026 (day 0); oil reacted on 2 March. Pre-event information (tensions in February) may leak into earlier days; so the estimation window ends 31 trading days before day 0.
**Method.** Market model on excess returns, R_it − RF_t = a_i + b_i (Mkt-RF)_t + e_it, estimated by OLS over trading days −260 to −31 relative to day 0. Abnormal return AR_it = actual − predicted. Cumulative abnormal return CAR_i[0,k] = Σ AR over days 0..k. Windows: k = 0, 1, 4 (first week; primary), 19 (first month), and through 31 Aug 2026 (≈ day 125). Check: three-factor model (adds SMB and HML).
**Inference.** For each industry, an empirical p-value: the share of all rolling 5-day windows within days −260..−31 whose |CAR| is at least |CAR_i[0,4]| (same for the other horizons with matching window lengths). Cross-industry multiplicity: the rank of |CAR[0,4]| among the 48 industries, and a Holm-adjusted p for the 3 pre-specified primary industries.
**Primary hypotheses (directional, fixed now).** H_S1: Petroleum and Natural Gas (FF "Oil") CAR[0,4] > 0. H_S2: Transportation (FF "Trans": airlines, railroads, trucking, shipping) CAR[0,4] < 0. H_S3: Restaurants, hotels, motels (FF "Meals") CAR[0,4] < 0.
**Secondary (descriptive).** All 48 industries; groups: consumer discretionary (Autos, Fun, Rtail, Hshld, Clths, Toys), staples (Food, Soda, Beer, Smoke), Utilities, Banks, Aero/Defense ("Guns"), Gold, and the market itself (Mkt-RF CAR).
**Limitations.** Daily industry portfolios mix many firms; stock reactions reflect expectations about future cash flows, not realized sales; other news on the same days (tariffs, rates) cannot be separated; a single event; the market model's beta is estimated on a calm year; the post-event horizon is not a clean causal window beyond about a week.
