# Semiconductor stocks and the 2026 Iran war (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`; `analysis_semis.py`; `results_semis.json`)
Yahoo Finance daily closes (local dates) to 13–14 Oct 2026. Abnormal returns: U.S. stocks vs the S&P 500 (market model, 3 Mar 2025–30 Jan 2026); Asian stocks vs the previous U.S. session's S&P 500 return. Empirical p from rolling windows of the estimation period.
**Part 1. Onset (CAR over the first 5 local trading days after the 28 Feb weekend; first day 2 March, Korea 3 March).**
- **Korea: −23.0% Samsung (p < 0.01), −24.8% SK hynix (p < 0.01)**, KOSPI −17.6% (p < 0.01). Taiwan: TSMC −5.0% (p = 0.08), TAIEX −4.7% (p = 0.04). Japan: Nikkei −5.3% (p = 0.02), Tokyo Electron −4.8% (p = 0.39).
- U.S.: SOX −4.2% (p = 0.09); TSMC (ADR) −7.8% (p = 0.01), Micron −8.6% (p = 0.22), Intel −2.8%, AMD −1.0%, Qualcomm −1.1%, but **Nvidia +3.6% and Broadcom +6.5%** (not significant). U.S. semiconductors were mixed in week one and the SOX index recovered over the first 20 days (+3.9%, p = 0.36); Korean and Taiwanese chips did not (−24% and −6% over 20 days).
**Part 2. Path since the 27 Feb close.** SOX **+50.8%** (S&P 500 +13.5%, Nasdaq +20.0%); trough −11.8% on 30 March (S&P −7.8%), peak +80.7% on 22 June. AMD +202%, Micron +131%, Intel +125%, Nvidia +29%, Broadcom +12%, Qualcomm +24%, TSMC ADR +19%. Taiwan: TSMC +27%, TAIEX +38%. Korea: SK hynix +38.5% (peak +175% on 22 June), Samsung +10% (peak +67% on 18 June), KOSPI −1.7% (peak +46% on 22 June; trough −19% on 31 March). Japan: Tokyo Electron +32% (peak +79% on 1 July), Nikkei +16%. Many series peaked on 22 June (SOX, Intel, SK hynix, KOSPI) and fell afterward (Korean chips −13% on 23 June).
**Part 3. Transmission from the previous U.S. SOX session.** Samsung β 0.37 → 0.62 (difference p = 0.22), SK hynix 0.60 → 0.83 (p = 0.29), TSMC Taiwan 0.41 → 0.34 (p = 0.41), KOSPI 0.29 → 0.55 (p = 0.085): the rise in sensitivity to the SOX is weaker than the rise in sensitivity to the S&P 500 reported in `../korea/RESULTS.md` (KOSPI 0.58 → 1.77).
**Part 4. Trump-statement events (12).** SOX days 0–1: mean |AR| 2.89% vs 2.60% on random windows (p = 0.27); threats mean +0.77% (not negative; p = 0.74); call-offs/deals +2.29% (p = 0.076 for positive). Nvidia: 1.38% vs 1.84% (p = 0.86). Korea next-day: Samsung 4.15% vs 4.11% (p = 0.46), threats −1.88% (p = 0.21), call-offs +1.55% (p = 0.24); SK hynix 5.90% vs 4.82% (p = 0.19), TSMC (Taiwan) 1.77% vs 1.46% (p = 0.19). 5 of the 20 largest |SOX abnormal| days since 2 March are within ±1 day of these events, against about 4 expected (events cover 20% of days).
**Reading.** The war's first-week shock hit semiconductors in Asia hard (Korean chips −23% to −25%, Taiwan and Japan −5%) and less in the U.S.; the global semiconductor complex then recovered and far outperformed the market (SOX +51%), in a way that tracks the AI cycle more than the war. Trump-statement days show no significant excess semiconductor reaction, except a marginal positive reaction of the SOX to call-offs/deals. The 22 June peak and the following Korean drop (−10.5% KOSPI on 23 June) are the most notable sequence, but the data do not show whether the statement caused them.
