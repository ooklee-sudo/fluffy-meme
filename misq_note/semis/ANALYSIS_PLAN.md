# Semiconductor stocks and the 2026 Iran war: analysis plan (fixed before computing)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14 at the author's request ("look only at semiconductors"). Disclosure: earlier analyses already showed Samsung Electronics and SK hynix returns on selected days (e.g., 3–5 March and 22–23 June 2026, cumulative +9.9% and +38.7% since 27 Feb), Nvidia and Broadcom returns on the 14 big-tech event days, and that Korean markets became more sensitive to U.S. moves; no other semiconductor series (SOX, TSMC, AMD, Micron, Intel, Taiwan, Japan) has been examined.
**Data.** Yahoo Finance daily closes (local dates), 2 Jan 2025–14 Oct 2026: PHLX Semiconductor index (^SOX), NVDA, AVGO, AMD, MU, INTC, TSM, QCOM; Taiwan: TSMC (2330.TW), TAIEX (^TWII); Korea: Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, KOSPI; Japan: Nikkei 225 and Tokyo Electron (8035.T); S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite.
**Part 1: onset.** Market model on the local broad index (S&P 500 for U.S. stocks and SOX; for Asian markets, the previous-U.S.-session S&P 500 return plus the local index return is not used; instead abnormal return = return minus (a + b·previous U.S. session S&P 500 return), as in `../korea/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`), estimated 3 Mar 2025–30 Jan 2026. CAR over the first 5 local trading days after the weekend of 28 Feb (U.S.: 2–6 March; Taiwan: 2–6 March; Korea: 3–9 March because of the 2 March holiday; Japan: 2–6 March) and over the first 20 trading days. Hypothesis H1: semiconductors fell on the onset (CAR[0,4] < 0) in the U.S., Taiwan, Korea, and Japan.
**Part 2: path.** Cumulative return from the 27 Feb close to 13 Oct for each series and its excess over the local broad index; maximum drawdown after 2 March; peak-to-date.
**Part 3: transmission.** r_Korean chip,t = a + b·r_SOX,prev(t) + e, pre-war vs. since 2 March (HAC 5 lags, interaction test), for Samsung and SK hynix; same for TSMC/Taiwan on SOX (previous U.S. session).
**Part 4: Trump-statement events.** The 12 events in `../bigtech/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`; the abnormal return of SOX (vs S&P 500) in the U.S. on the event window [0,+1] and of Samsung/SK hynix on the next Korean trading day; magnitude test against random days since 2 March (permutation, 5,000) and signed tests for threats (negative expected) and call-offs/deals (positive expected). Also the share of the 20 largest |SOX abnormal return| days since 2 March that are within ±1 day of those events.
**Limitations.** Semiconductor stocks respond to many non-war news items (AI demand, export controls, earnings); the price series are daily closes (not total returns); Part 4 repeats the 12 news-selected events.
