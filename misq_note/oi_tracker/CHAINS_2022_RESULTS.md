# Fast-food chains' same-store sales around the 2022 gasoline surge (pre-registered, `ANALYSIS_PLAN_CHAINS_2022.md`; script `analysis_chains2022.py`; output `results_chains2022.json`)
Data: 12 chains (CAVA not public until 2023, so excluded for 2022), 59 new rows for calendar 2021Q4–2022Q4 from SEC 8-K Exhibit 99.1; all quotes verified as exact substrings of the cited exhibits, comps values found in their quotes, URLs belong to the right company (`verify_quotes_2022.py`, `analysis_chains2022.py`); fiscal quarters mapped to calendar quarters by the midpoint rule. McDonald's was extracted by me directly from EDGAR, the other 11 by subagents. Notes: Yum 2021Q4 is the Taco Bell Division figure (no US-only bullet); Starbucks, Yum, Sweetgreen, Papa John's figures are whole-number percents; Wingstop Q4 2022 excludes the 53rd week; Chipotle is not US-labelled; reference quarters 2023Q2–2025Q4 use the existing, verified extraction. McDonald's has no calendar-2022Q4 row in this merge (not used in the tests below). Quarters were not checked for the Wingstop Q1/Q2 2022 fiscal end dates, which the agent inferred (no effect on calendar mapping).

**H_G1 is not supported.**
- Primary Δ_2022Q2 (2022Q2 minus 2022Q1, mean over 12 chains) = **−1.70 pp**; the 11 reference quarters 2023Q2–2025Q4 range from −3.65 to +1.85; the shock quarter is 4th lowest of 12, one-sided rank p = 0.33; cross-chain t = −1.02, p = 0.33; 5 of 12 chains decreased (Sweetgreen −19.0, Wingstop −4.5, Starbucks −3.0, Papa John's −1.0; Yum +3.0, Wendy's +1.2, Chipotle +1.1).
- Secondary Δ_2022Q3 = **+1.07 pp** (rank 11 of 12 from the lowest; p = 0.92): growth recovered in the quarter after the gasoline peak.
- Descriptive: the cross-chain mean comps was 11.2% (2021Q4), 6.2% (2022Q1), 4.5% (2022Q2), 5.5% (2022Q3), 5.9% (2022Q4), 9.1% (2023Q1). The largest quarterly drop was 2022Q1 (−5.0 pp), the quarter before gasoline reached its peak (Omicron, fading 2021 base), not 2022Q2.

**Reading.** Chain growth slowed from late 2021 to mid-2022, but the quarter with peak gasoline prices (2022Q2) is not unusual relative to other quarters, and the following quarter rebounded. Base effects (2021 reopening), menu-price inflation and Omicron make 2022 comps hard to read; one-sided p = 0.33 with 11 reference quarters has little power. Sweetgreen's fall (35% → 16%) is largely a base effect of its 2021 reopening and dominates the mean (excluding it the mean change would be positive; not a pre-registered check).
