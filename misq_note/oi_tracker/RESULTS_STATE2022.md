# 2022 gasoline surge × state driving exposure, OI/Affinity card spending (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN_STATE_2022.md`; script `analysis_state2022.py`; outputs `results_state2022*.json`)
Panel: 50 states × 154 weeks (DC dropped: coverage < 90%), 2021-07-11 to 2024-06-16; E1 = 2019 VMT per resident (standardized), E2 = 2019 poverty rate. β = effect of (exposure × ln gas price) on spending, percentage points of the Jan-2020 baseline per SD per log-point; state and week fixed effects; state-clustered SEs; permutation p from 1,000 draws (planned 5,000; reduced for run time).

**W1 (primary) not supported.** Accommodation/food service (acf) × E1: β = −0.008 (se 0.011, cluster p = 0.47; permutation two-sided 0.52, lower-tail 0.26).
**W2 not supported (sign opposite).** Discretionary composite β = +0.027 (p = 0.11; perm 0.10); apparel −0.024 (p = 0.18); arts/entertainment/recreation **+0.105 (p = 0.005; perm 0.006)**; sporting goods +0.034 (p = 0.22). High-driving states raised, not cut, recreation spending when gasoline rose.
**W3 (essential ≥ 0) holds.** ESS +0.014 (p = 0.31); grocery +0.023 (p = 0.16; perm 0.11); health care +0.004. Total spending +0.015 (p = 0.18).
**W4 (income) not supported.** Poverty × gas on acf +0.002 (p = 0.90), DISC +0.022 (p = 0.17); on ESS +0.026 (p < 0.001, perm 0.002, i.e., poorer states increased essential spending when gas rose). VMT × gas on total spending: q1 ZIPs +0.017 (p = 0.19), q4 +0.006 (p = 0.52); difference not tested formally.
**Checks.** acf: dropping DC/AK/HI −0.005 (p = 0.63); dropping five largest states −0.005 (p = 0.65); sample to 2023-06 −0.011 (p = 0.32). The only specification with a significant negative acf estimate is the 8-week gasoline-change dose (dG8): −0.028 (p = 0.031); with the same dose DISC −0.011 (p = 0.58), ESS +0.010 (p = 0.36). Event study for acf: the pre-period bins are small and jointly marginal (Wald p = 0.099); the post bins are positive (about +0.005 to +0.014), not negative.

**Reading.** In state-level card data, 2022 gasoline prices did not reduce food-service or discretionary spending more in high-driving states, and one component (recreation) moved the opposite way; one of five checks (the 8-week change dose) gives a small negative for accommodation/food service only. Pattern is consistent with the national and Iran-war results: no discretionary retrenchment.
