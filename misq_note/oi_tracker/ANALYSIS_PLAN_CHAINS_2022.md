# Fast-food chains' same-store sales during the 2022 gasoline surge: analysis plan (fixed before any 2022 chain figure is extracted)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14, after the author asked to examine fast-food chain sales for the 2022–23 gasoline episode. For 2023Q1 onward the same 13 chains' comps are already extracted (`../oil_income/chains/*.json`, quotes verified) and were used in the 2026 analysis; no 2022 figure has been extracted or seen. Chain comps for 2022 were not seen before (press recollection aside, none used).

**Shock.** National regular gasoline (FRED GASREGW) averaged roughly $3.4 in 2021Q4, rose after Russia's invasion of Ukraine (24 Feb 2022) to a mid-June 2022 peak, then fell back by 2022Q4. Calendar 2022Q2 is the primary shock quarter (peak prices), 2022Q3 secondary, 2022Q1 transitional (late-quarter increase).

**Data.** Same source and rules as `../oil_income/ANALYSIS_PLAN_CHAINS.md`: US same-store/comparable sales growth (year over year) from earnings 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (SEC EDGAR) for the 13 chains (McDonald's US, Wendy's US, RBI Burger King US, Yum Taco Bell US, Domino's US, Wingstop domestic, Chipotle, Starbucks US, Jack in the Box brand, Papa John's North America, Shake Shack same-Shack, CAVA, Sweetgreen), for calendar quarters 2021Q4 to 2022Q4, mapped to calendar quarters by the quarter containing the fiscal quarter's midpoint (fiscal quarter end minus 45 days). CAVA (IPO June 2023) and Sweetgreen (IPO Nov 2021) may have no figures; chains with missing quarters are dropped from the affected Δ only. Each figure carries its quote and URL and is checked programmatically against the exhibit text.

**Primary outcome and test.** Δ_{c,t} = comps_{c,t} − comps_{c,t−1}; Δ_t = unweighted mean over chains with both quarters. Primary statistic Δ_2022Q2 (2022Q2 minus 2022Q1). Reference distribution: Δ_t for t = 2023Q2..2025Q4 (11 quarters, existing data) plus the shock quarter; one-sided rank p (share of the 12 at most Δ_2022Q2); minimum 1/12 = 0.083. Hypothesis H_G1: the average chain's comps growth falls from 2022Q1 to 2022Q2 by more than in the reference quarters (supported only if p ≤ 0.10).
**Secondary.** (a) Δ_2022Q3 on the same reference distribution (note: 2022Q3 reference excludes itself). (b) Level shift: mean comps in 2022Q2 minus the mean of 2021Q4 and 2022Q1 (two preceding quarters) is not used because 2021 base effects dominate; instead report cross-chain t-test of Δ_{c,2022Q2} and share of chains with a decrease. (c) Year-ago comparison: comps_{2022Q2} − comps_{2021Q2} is not defined for lack of 2021Q2; omitted.

**Known confounds (stated in advance).** 2022 comps are year-over-year against a 2021 base inflated by reopening and stimulus (strong base effects that mechanically reduce 2022 growth through the year), and menu-price inflation raised nominal comps in 2022. The test therefore compares the change in comps growth with changes in other years, not an absolute effect. Delivery/third-party and traffic effects are not separated. Chain composition: 13 listed chains.

**Interpretation rules.** As in the 2026 chain plan; a non-significant result is not evidence of no effect. A negative result would not be attributed to gasoline prices alone (inflation, Omicron, labor shortages in 2022).
