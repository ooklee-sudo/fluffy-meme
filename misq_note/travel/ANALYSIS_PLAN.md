# 2026 Iran war and U.S. citizens' international travel: analysis plan (fixed before the series is examined, with one disclosure)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14 at the author's request (the 2026 war should have reduced Americans' travel abroad). **Disclosure:** while inspecting the workbook layout, the last rows of the 2026 sheet were printed, which include the 2026/2025 percent changes for the Middle East (Jan +12%, Feb 0%, Mar −55%, Apr −48%, May −24%) and for overseas total and grand total (roughly +1% to +5%). Those were seen; no other region's levels or changes, and no historical level, were examined. The Middle East test is therefore confirmatory only in the sense that its size is compared with placebo years; the other regions are not prejudged.

**Data.** NTTO, "U.S. citizen travel to international regions" (workbook `US-Outbound-to-World-Regions.xlsx`, trade.gov, released 2 September 2026): monthly U.S. citizen departures by region of destination. Overseas and Mexico-air counts come from DHS APIS (I-92; non-stop flights from a U.S. port to a foreign port); Mexico (total) and Canada (total) include land and are from INEGI and Statistics Canada. Regions: Europe, Caribbean, Asia, South America, Central America, Oceania, Middle East, Africa; Total Overseas; Mexico (Air and Total); Canada (Air and Total). Data through June 2026 (preliminary). Annual sheets 2009–2026 (first table of each sheet; the same table in 2020 is "2020rev").

**Shock.** War onset 28 Feb 2026; Post = March–June 2026 (4 months).
**Method (identical to `../oil_income/ANALYSIS_PLAN_MRTS.md`).** For year Y fit on the 36 months before March of Y: ln N_t = month effects + linear trend; forecast March–June of Y; D_Y = mean (actual − forecast) in logs. Placebo years: 2012–2019 and 2025 (training windows free of the COVID period, 2025 uses Mar 2022–Feb 2025). Permutation p: share of the ten years with |D_Y| ≥ |D_2026|; one-sided low-tail p: share of the ten at most D_2026 (minimum 0.10). Because the Middle East series has had large shocks in placebo years (e.g., 2020-01 and June-2025 events could fall in the windows), those windows are not removed.
**Hypotheses.**
- T1 (primary, directional): D_2026 < 0 for the Middle East, ranked among the lowest of the ten years.
- T2 (spillover, directional): D_2026 < 0 for total overseas excluding the Middle East (fuel prices, airfares, security concerns), and for Europe and Asia separately.
- T3 (substitution): D_2026 ≥ 0 for Caribbean, Mexico (total) and Canada (total) (nearby, drivable or short-haul destinations).
**Checks fixed in advance.** Training windows 24 and 48 months; no trend; Post = March–May only; the same calculation for January–February 2026 as a pre-war check (should be near zero); Africa and Central America as non-war regions.
**Limitations.** Citizens' departures are a count of travelers, not a price-adjusted measure; APIS counts are nonstop flights to foreign ports (a Middle East trip by connection via Europe is counted in Europe); some Mexico/Canada totals are preliminary and from other agencies; Middle East volumes are small and volatile; four post months; ten reference years; the war's effect on airspace, airline schedules, and U.S. government travel advisories cannot be separated from demand.
