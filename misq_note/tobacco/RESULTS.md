# Tobacco taxable removals after the March 2026 war onset (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`; `analysis_tobacco.py`; `results_tobacco.json`)
TTB national monthly data, Jan 2012–Jun 2026; tax-paid removals (wholesale shipments). Rolling-origin deviation, Post = Mar–Jun 2026; reference years 2015–2019 and 2025 (6, plus 2026 = 7; minimum attainable p ≈ 0.14).
- **Cigarettes: −1.0% vs trend** (perm p = 0.86; placebo range −8.3% to +8.7%). Training 24 months +4.3%, 48 months −3.6%, March–May +0.2%; no-trend model −23.6% (this only reflects the secular decline). The year-over-year change in cigarette removals was −22% in Jan, −17% in Feb (before the war), −15% in Mar, −3% in Apr, −16% in May, −15% in Jun 2026: no break at the war onset; the decline predates it.
- Other products (descriptive, small categories): roll-your-own +13% (p = 0.67), snuff +7.7% (p = 0.14), chewing +8.5% (p = 0.17), pipe +6.1% (p = 0.71); none distinguishable from the placebo years with seven years of reference. The cigar categories could not be parsed from this file (detail names did not match in the first pass) and are not reported.
**Reading.** No evidence that cigarette shipments changed after the war began; the structural decline (about 15–20% per year in this file's 2026 months) continued. Removals are not retail sales and cannot show inventory timing, and excise/state tax changes are not controlled.
