# Tobacco removals (taxable) after the March 2026 war onset: analysis plan (fixed before the series is examined)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14 at the author's request. Only the file structure (columns, category names, last month) was inspected; no value of any tobacco series was examined.
**Data.** TTB Tobacco Statistics, national monthly file `Tobacco_Products_monthly_data_csv.csv` (Treasury Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau; manufacturer reports on Form TTB F 5210.5), January 2012–June 2026 (file marked updated 25 Aug 2026). Series: "Removed Taxable including from Puerto Rico" (tax-paid removals from manufacturers/importers = wholesale shipments, the closest public monthly proxy for consumption) for: cigarettes (number), small and large cigars (number), roll-your-own tobacco, snuff, chewing tobacco, pipe tobacco (pounds). Removals lead consumer purchases by weeks and can reflect inventory timing (pre-buying before tax changes).
**Method (as in `../oil_income/ANALYSIS_PLAN_MRTS.md`).** Rolling-origin deviation D_Y: fit ln N_t = month effects + linear trend on the 36 months before March of Y, forecast March–June of Y (four post months; June 2026 is the last observation), D_Y = mean(actual − forecast). Placebo years: 2012... but with training windows needing 36 months of history, the available placebo years are 2015–2019 and 2025 (2012–2014 lack 36 pre-months in this file; COVID years excluded). Permutation p = share of the seven years (six placebo + 2026) with |D_Y| ≥ |D_2026| (minimum 1/7); one-sided low-tail p reported.
**Hypotheses.** No directional claim for cigarettes (income effect could reduce quantity; stress could raise it); two-sided. Primary: cigarettes. Secondary: the other products (descriptive).
**Checks fixed in advance.** Training 24 and 48 months; no trend; Post = March–May; the share of cigars within all taxable removals.
**Limitations.** Removals are not retail sales; the secular decline in cigarettes (about 5–8% a year) is absorbed by the trend but not constant; seven reference years only (minimum p = 0.14); excise tax or state-law changes can create shifts not related to the war; four post months; redactions for small categories.
