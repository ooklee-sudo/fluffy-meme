# Weekly Iran-attack signals and market reactions: analysis plan (fixed before computing)

**Status.** Written 2026-10-14. The weekly signal counts were already computed (`results_poisson.json`: S1 108 posts, S2 78, bursts in mid-March and mid-April) and the earlier big-tech event study found no excess reaction on 14 news-selected days. No weekly market return was examined.
**Data.** Weekly counts of S1 and S2 signals (definitions in `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`) over 32 weeks, Monday 2 March–Sunday 11 October 2026 (weeks by U.S. trading week, using UTC post dates mapped to the Monday–Sunday week). Markets: WTI crude oil (FRED DCOILWTICO, weekly average change in log price, and Friday-to-Friday change), S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, equal-weighted return of the eight big-tech stocks and its abnormal return (market model on the S&P 500 estimated 3 Mar 2025–30 Jan 2026), all from the earlier price file (Yahoo).
**Models (primary).** For each outcome y_w (weekly Friday-to-Friday log return in percent; WTI log change in percent): y_w = a + b·signals_w + e_w, OLS with Newey–West (2 lags) standard errors. Hypotheses: b > 0 for WTI (threats raise oil), b < 0 for the S&P 500, Nasdaq, big-tech portfolio (threats lower stocks). Secondary: S2; an indicator for "high-signal weeks" (top quartile of weekly count) versus other weeks (difference in means with permutation p, 5,000 draws); lag-one signals (do signals predict next week's return?); the same on daily data (daily count vs daily returns, 227 days, HAC 5 lags).
**Interpretation.** A signal effect is supported only if the sign matches and the HAC p < 0.05 for at least WTI and one equity outcome; with 32 weeks and 8 outcomes, p-values will be reported unadjusted and Holm-adjusted across the four primary outcomes.
**Limitations.** Reverse causality (signals respond to market and oil moves, which are also driven by actual events), keyword-based signal definition, short sample (32 weeks), strongly auto-correlated news flow, and the fact that actual military actions and Iranian responses are not controlled.
