# Weekly Iran-attack signals vs oil and stock returns (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN_MARKETS.md`; `analysis_markets.py`; `results_markets.json`)
31 complete trading weeks (2 March–2 October 2026; the plan said 32, the last week 5–11 October is incomplete and was dropped). Weekly outcomes: WTI log change, S&P 500, Nasdaq, eight big-tech stocks (equal-weighted) and their abnormal return vs the S&P 500, all in percent (sum of daily log changes); signals: S1 (S2) post counts in the same Mon–Sun week.
- **Primary (S1, contemporaneous, Newey–West 2 lags): no relation.** Each extra signal in a week: WTI +0.21 pp (p = 0.72), S&P 500 +0.09 (p = 0.54), Nasdaq +0.19 (p = 0.31), big-tech portfolio +0.18 (p = 0.42), big-tech abnormal +0.04 (p = 0.53). The signs for the stock outcomes are positive, opposite to the hypothesis (threats lower stocks). Holm-adjusted p ≈ 1 for all four primary outcomes.
- **S2:** WTI −0.54 (p = 0.42), S&P +0.18 (p = 0.28), Nasdaq +0.31 (p = 0.16), tech +0.26 (p = 0.40), tech abnormal −0.01 (p = 0.93).
- **Lagged signals** (do last week's signals predict this week?): all p > 0.37.
- **High-signal weeks** (top quartile, 12 weeks) vs others: WTI +2.6 pp (perm p = 0.52), S&P +0.7 (0.31), Nasdaq +1.2 (0.23), tech +0.7 (0.59), tech abnormal −0.3 (0.67).
- **Daily** (157 days): S1 on WTI +0.14 (p = 0.75), S&P +0.02 (p = 0.84), Nasdaq +0.04 (p = 0.73), tech +0.03 (p = 0.80), tech abnormal +0.005 (p = 0.93).
**Reading.** Weeks and days with more Trump attack-threat posts were not associated with higher oil or lower stock prices; in the weekly data the largest oil and equity swings (e.g., the first week of March with WTI +30% and S&P −2%; the April weeks with S&P +3% to +4% and WTI −14%) occurred with ordinary or moderately high signal counts, and actual events (strikes, the Hormuz closure, the ceasefire, the memorandum) dominate prices. The null is not evidence that statements are irrelevant: signals are keyword-defined, partly reactions to markets and events, and the sample is 31 weeks.
