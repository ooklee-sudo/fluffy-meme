# Are Trump's Iran-attack signals Poisson? (plan `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`; `analysis_poisson.py`; `results_poisson.json`)
Data: 4,838 original Truth Social posts, 1 Mar–13 Oct 2026 (CNN archive; pure re-posts excluded); 373 mention Iran; S1 (Iran + threat term): 108 posts on 70 of 227 days (mean 0.48/day); S2 (+ forward-looking modality): 78 posts (0.34/day).
**Homogeneous Poisson is rejected (S1; S2 similar).**
- Daily counts: variance 0.74 vs mean 0.48; dispersion index 350 on 226 df (χ² p = 2e-7; parametric bootstrap p < 0.001); negative binomial fits far better (size ≈ 0.79; LR p = 3e-6). Weekly counts: mean 3.25, variance 11.3, index 108 on 31 df (p = 2e-10).
- Inter-arrival times: mean 51 h, median 21 h, coefficient of variation 1.94 (Poisson/exponential: 1), KS test against the exponential p = 0.0001: bursty arrivals.
- Clustering: daily autocorrelation 0.39 at lag 1, 0.23 at lag 2; mean next-day count 0.83 after a signal day versus 0.31 after a quiet day; signals appear on 31% of days.
**But a Poisson process with a time-varying rate fits.** With week fixed effects, the Pearson dispersion of daily counts is 0.89 (S1) and 0.90 (S2) (≈ 1 = Poisson). I.e., the data look like a non-homogeneous (Cox) Poisson process: counts within a week are roughly Poisson, but the weekly rate changes a lot (bursts: 11–14 March and 11–22 April account for the busiest days: 14 Mar 5 posts, 12 and 21 April 4 each).
**Link to the stock-market events.** 22% (S1) and 27% (S2) of signals fall within ±1 day of the 14 news-derived events, versus 17% of all days within ±1 day of an event, i.e., only mildly concentrated; most signals do not coincide with those events.
**Reading.** Not a simple Poisson process; it is better described as a Poisson process whose intensity varies over time (overdispersed, negative-binomial counts) with news-driven bursts. This is the same Poisson–Gamma structure as the review-count model in this project. Caveats: keyword classification of what counts as an 'attack signal', Truth Social only, UTC dates.
