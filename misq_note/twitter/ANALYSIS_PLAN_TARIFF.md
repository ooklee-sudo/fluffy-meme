# Pre-registration: information-theoretic propositions on tariff/trade/China tweets, 2017–2021 (committed before labelling, tick download or return computation, 2026-10-14)

Same logic as ANALYSIS_PLAN_TWITTER.md (oil/Iran/energy sample, still in progress); this is a second, independent confirmatory sample with a different asset. Already seen: only the number of keyword tweets by year (937 in the frame: 2017 86, 2018 276, 2019 363, 2020 210, 2021 2; 632 after the filter below). Not seen: any price, return, label or novelty score for these tweets. Prior knowledge: the Truth Social tariff results (RESULTS_PAPER.md: hourly S&P futures; tariff escalation after April 2025 −0.35% directional, p = .09) and the oil-sample propositions.

## Frame (fixed by rule)
Original tweets (not starting with "RT @") by @realDonaldTrump, 2017-01-20 to 2021-01-08, containing (case-insensitive) tariff*, trade, duties, duty, nafta, usmca, korus, wto, dumping, steel, aluminum, aluminium, china, chinese, beijing, xi, huawei, zte, or "currency manipulat*" (`frame_tariff.csv`, 937 tweets). Exact UTC time from the snowflake id. Cluster filter: keep a tweet if at least 3 hours have passed since the previous frame tweet (632 kept). A tweet that also belongs to the oil/Iran frame stays in both analyses.

## Market data
Primary asset: Dukascopy S&P 500 index CFD (`USA500IDXUSD`; raw price/1000), mid price; price at s = last tick at or before s, valid only if at most 10 minutes old. Secondary asset: USD/CNH (`USDCNH`; raw/100000). Daily S&P 500 closes (Yahoo ^GSPC, `spx_daily.csv`) for scaling. An event needs valid prices at t−30, t and t+30 minutes.

## Measures
- |z| = |100·ln(P(t+30)/P(t))| / s30, s30 = σ_d × sqrt(30/1380), σ_d = sd (in percent) of the 60 daily S&P log returns before the tweet date; U = ln(sd of the 5 daily returns / sd of the 60); novelty N_c as in ANALYSIS_PLAN_INFO.md with a 7-day context of all original tweets (computed for the frame text only, no returns); robustness N_t.
- Directional convention: tariff escalation (ESC) → S&P down, so the directional S&P return is −r; tariff de-escalation (DEESC) → S&P up (+r). For USD/CNH: ESC → up (+), DEESC → down (−).
- Labels (two independent model instances, text only; humans on a subset): ESC = announces, threatens, imposes or celebrates tariffs, duties, quotas, trade restrictions, export or sanction measures on firms, or retaliation; criticises a trade partner in a way that signals coming trade action; demands backed by a threat of such measures. DEESC = announces or signals a trade deal or agreement, tariff reduction, pause, delay, exemption or exclusion, progress in talks, a meeting to resolve a dispute, purchase commitments by a trade partner, or relief on restrictions. MIXED = both clearly. OTHER = no implication for the course of trade policy: general statements on the economy or jobs, complaints about trade deficits without an announced action, praise of own policy, attacks on media or opponents, unrelated mentions (e.g., China outside trade), a bare link or no stated stance.

## Hypotheses (confirmatory; decision rules fixed now)
- **HI1–HI3** exactly as in ANALYSIS_PLAN_TWITTER.md (|z| on N_c, U, N_c × U; year fixed effects; clustered by UTC day; one-sided; Holm across the three at α = .05), on all kept frame tweets with valid windows (label-free).
- **H10:** among tweets with an agreed model label DEESC the mean directional 30-minute S&P return (+r) > 0; among ESC the mean (−r) > 0; one-sided Wilcoxon, α = .05; same on the human-labelled subset when available (primary if at least 20 human-labelled tweets in the class have windows); paired post-30 vs pre-30 comparison as in H7b.
- **H11 (secondary asset):** the same H10 tests for USD/CNH on the subset of tweets that mention china, chinese, beijing, xi, huawei, zte or "currency" (any case).
- **Power statement:** about 400–500 events with valid windows are expected; the standard error of the N_c coefficient is expected around 0.2–0.4 |z| units; effects below about 0.5–0.8 cannot be excluded.

## Reporting
RESULTS_TARIFF.md; code `build_frame_tariff.py`, `analysis_tariff.py`; output `results_tariff.json`. Deviations go to the amendments log. Neither positive nor negative results are suppressed.

## Amendments
**Amendment 1 (2026-10-14; before any tariff-sample price or return has been examined; the tariff labels from two model instances already exist, no returns do).** A state-dependent sign proposition (P6) is added as a confirmatory hypothesis. Rationale: in the oil/Iran Twitter sample the direction of the response (escalation responded, de-escalation did not) is the reverse of the 2026 closure sample; an information-theoretic reading says a message is more informative when it runs against the market's prior over the state, so the response to a class of message should be larger when the recent regime is the opposite of that class. That reading of the oil results is post hoc; the tariff sample is its pre-registered test.
- **Regime variable R** (labels only, no returns): for each kept tweet at time t, R = (nESC − nDEESC) / (nESC + nDEESC), counted over all frame tweets in the 30 days before t whose two model labels agree on ESC or DEESC (the tweet itself excluded); R is missing if fewer than 3 such tweets exist. R > 0 means the recent regime is escalatory (hawkish), R < 0 de-escalatory.
- **Hypothesis H12:** among kept tweets with an agreed model label ESC or DEESC and a valid S&P tick window and R, let dir30 be the directional 30-minute S&P return (+r for DEESC, −r for ESC) and s = +1 for DEESC, −1 for ESC. Estimate dir30 = α_class + β · (s × R) + ε by OLS with a class-specific intercept, standard errors clustered by UTC day. **H12: β > 0** (a de-escalation message moves the market more when the recent regime is escalatory, and an escalation message moves it more when the recent regime is de-escalatory). One-sided, α = .05. Supported only if p < .05; otherwise reported as not supported. The same test is repeated on the human-labelled subset when available (primary if at least 40 human-labelled signal tweets with R).
- **Secondary (descriptive):** the mean directional return of each class in the tercile of R most opposed to the class and in the tercile most aligned with it; the same H12 specification for USD/CNH on the China-related subset (H11 set); and, **exploratory and not confirmatory because its outcomes are already known**, the same specification on the oil/Iran Twitter sample and on the Truth Social samples.
- H12 is counted separately from HI1–HI3 (Holm applies within HI1–HI3 only).

**Amendment 2 (2026-10-14; before any SOX return was computed for this sample).** S&P 500 remains the primary asset and every S&P result stays reported. The results already seen are the S&P results in RESULTS_TARIFF.md (HI1–HI3, H10, H12); no SOX or semiconductor return has been examined for the 2017–2021 tweets. A semiconductor channel is added as a secondary, pre-registered test because trade measures aimed at China (export controls, ZTE and Huawei restrictions, tariffs on electronics) bear more directly on semiconductors than on the index.
- **Asset and abnormal return.** Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (^SOX) and S&P 500 (^GSPC) daily closes (Yahoo; `sox_daily.csv`, `spx_daily.csv`). Daily log returns r in percent. Abnormal semiconductor return A = r_SOX − β̂ × r_SPX, β̂ from an OLS of daily r_SOX on r_SPX over the 250 trading days ending the day before the reaction day (β̂ = 1 if fewer than 150 observations).
- **Event mapping (fixed now).** Tweet time converted to America/New_York. Outside regular hours (before 09:30 on a trading day, from 16:00, or on a weekend or holiday) the reaction day is the next regular session (the trading day itself if the tweet is before 09:30 on a trading day, otherwise the next trading day), and the reaction is that day's close-to-close A (previous close to close, so it contains the overnight gap and the session). Tweets published during regular hours (09:30–16:00 on a trading day) are excluded from H13 because a daily return mixes pre- and post-tweet movement; they are counted and reported.
- **Sample.** Kept tariff-frame tweets with an agreed model label ESC or DEESC (`labels_tariff_1.json`, `labels_tariff_2.json`). Several tweets on the same reaction day with the same class are collapsed to one observation per (reaction day, class). China subset: text containing china, chinese, beijing, xi, huawei, zte, or currency.
- **Directional convention.** Directional abnormal return = −A for ESC and +A for DEESC (semiconductors fall relative to the market after escalation, rise after de-escalation).
- **H13 (primary for this channel): in the China subset the mean directional abnormal return is positive;** one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on the (day, class) observations, α = .05. **H13b:** the same on all tariff-frame tweets. **H13c:** the semiconductor response exceeds the S&P response for the same events (paired difference of directional A and directional S&P daily return > 0, one-sided Wilcoxon), reported only if H13 or H13b is supported.
- **Sign-flip permutation** (10,000 draws, seed 20261014) of the event signs as a secondary check of H13 and H13b. Holm adjustment across H13 and H13b.
- **Exploratory, not confirmatory (outcomes of the Truth Social sample are already known):** the same directional abnormal return at the two-hour horizon from hourly SOX and S&P 500 bars for the tariff signal posts of 2025–2026 (`holdout_signal_posts.csv`, topic T, agreed labels).
- **Contingency for a tick design.** If H13 or H13b is supported at p < .10, a tick-level basket of individual semiconductor stock CFDs (US regular hours only) is registered in Amendment 3 before any of its data are downloaded; if neither is, no tick basket is built.
- Outputs: `analysis_sox.py`, `results_sox.json`, `RESULTS_SOX.md`.
