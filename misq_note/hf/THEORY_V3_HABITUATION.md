# Theory v3 (habituation / credibility learning) and an out-of-time confirmatory test (pre-registration; written 2026-10-14 before any 2022–2024 post is coded and before any return on them is computed)

**Where it comes from.** In the hourly data from October 2024 (`RESULTS_HOLDOUT.md`, exploratory), the price impact of a post fell with the number of same-topic signal posts in the previous 24 hours (pooled coefficient −0.24 on the standardized directional impact, p = 0.036; in-sample −0.28, p = 0.07; hold-out −0.16, p = 0.26). The 'attention' channel (7-day intensity) was not supported. This v3 states the habituation force as a theory and tests it on **different years** (14 Feb 2022 – 13 Oct 2024), which were not used to build it.
**Model.** Posts about a policy constraint (oil supply/geopolitics, tariffs) arrive as a Poisson process with Gamma-distributed intensity (supported: overdispersion). Investors hold a Beta prior on the 'follow-through probability' q that a threatened or promised policy change actually occurs. Each post is an announcement of the same kind; if prior similar posts in the recent window did not result in action, the posterior mean of q falls. The price impact of the next similar post is proportional to the posterior mean of q times the policy-relevant news value: ΔP = −β E[q | history] d. Hence:
- **H1 (habituation):** the standardized directional impact of a post decreases in the number of same-topic signal posts in the previous 24 hours (n24) and increases in the log time since the last such post.
- **H2 (first-in-cluster):** the first post after at least 24 hours of silence has a larger standardized directional impact than later posts.
- **H3 (credibility/ACTION):** the impact of ACTION-type posts decays less with n24 than that of RHETORIC posts (interaction n24 × ACTION > 0) — exploratory.
- **H4 (placebo):** posts classed OTHER show no dependence on n24.
**Design (out of time).** Posts from 14 Feb 2022 to 13 Oct 2024 matching (a) oil/geopolitics keywords (`iran|hormuz|russia|ukrain|putin|zelensk|venezuela|maduro|opec|saudi|libya|iraq|kuwait|israel|hamas|gaza|oil prices|oil|crude|gasoline|gas prices`) and a threat/peace term (`attack|strik|bomb|destroy|obliterat|annihilat|wipe out|blow up|kill|ultimatum|very hard|sanction|tariff|blockade|deal|ceasefire|agreement|peace|negotiat|talks|memorandum|reopen|open`) — 186 posts; (b) tariff/trade keywords — 48 posts. Text-free retweets excluded. Each post is coded blind by two independent model instances (same scheme as in `THEORY_AND_PAPER_PLAN.md`: direction ESC/DEESC/MIXED/OTHER by topic, type ACTION/RHETORIC); primary labels = coders agree.
**Outcomes.** Daily data only: WTI (FRED DCOILWTICO) for the oil topic, S&P 500 (Yahoo ^GSPC) for tariffs. Reaction day = the trading day on which the post is published during the New York session (09:30–16:00 ET), otherwise the next trading day; reaction = close-to-close log return of that day (percent); directional impact e as before (oil: ESC up, DEESC down; S&P: ESC down, DEESC up); standardized e_std = e / sd of daily returns over the previous 20 trading days. n24 = same-topic coded signal posts in the previous 24 hours (any non-OTHER class, agreed or not); gap = hours since the previous such post (cap 168 h).
**Primary confirmatory test.** Pooled regression of e_std on n24, log(1 + gap), topic × direction fixed effects, standard errors clustered by reaction day; the coefficient on n24 is predicted negative (H1); one-sided α = 0.05. If it fails, the habituation theory is reported as unsupported out of time.
**Secondary.** H2 (cluster-first dummy), H3 (interaction), H4 (OTHER posts' |z| on n24), by-topic estimates; leave-one-year-out.
**Power and limitations.** The expected number of signal posts after coding is around 60–100; daily returns contain all other news of the day (low signal-to-noise); posts in 2022–2024 are mostly by a private citizen/candidate (Trump was not president; market relevance is lower, and the effect size may therefore be smaller than in 2024–2026); the same-day dependence of posts is handled by clustering. A non-significant result is not evidence of no habituation because of low power; the minimum detectable effect will be reported.
