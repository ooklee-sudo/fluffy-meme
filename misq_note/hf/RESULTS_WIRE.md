# Who reported the decision first? (plan: ANALYSIS_PLAN_WIRE.md; code: analysis_wire.py; raw classifications qual/wire_A–D.json; output: results_wire.json)

All 61 war-period signal posts were classified by model-instance researchers who saw only the post time and text. Classes: POST_FIRST 32, OTHER_FIRST 14, SIMULTANEOUS 10, UNDETERMINABLE 5. POST_FIRST means no earlier public report was found, not that none exists: searches were keyword-based and several outlets (Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, Axios) could not be opened. SIMULTANEOUS (6 of 10 are 6–9 minutes after the post; 2 are Newsquawk relays of the post) means within ±10 minutes. Nine of the 14 OTHER_FIRST posts are links to articles (7) or re-posts (2); only 5 are original-text posts for which an outside source preceded the post.

Two-hour directional WTI reaction (%, sign applied): POST_FIRST mean +0.73 (median +0.24, n = 32); OTHER_FIRST +0.04 (median +0.11, n = 14); SIMULTANEOUS +1.07 (n = 10); UNDETERMINABLE +1.45 (n = 5).

**W1 (POST_FIRST larger than OTHER_FIRST): not supported.** Difference +0.70, one-sided permutation p = 0.176 (Mann-Whitney p = 0.150). De-escalation only: 1.38 versus 0.29 (n = 17 vs 10; p = 0.156). Escalation only: −0.00 versus −0.59 (n = 15 vs 4; p = 0.224). Exploratory sensitivities: counting SIMULTANEOUS as POST_FIRST (n = 42) difference +0.78, p = 0.154; excluding links and re-posts from OTHER_FIRST (n = 5) difference +0.60, p = 0.33. The pattern is in the predicted direction but the data cannot distinguish it from zero; the OTHER_FIRST class is dominated by content-free links, which is consistent with the redundancy argument but is not an independent test of it.
