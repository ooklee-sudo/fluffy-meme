# Korean airlines and the effect of oil, instrumented by Trump's de-escalation posts: plan (fixed before computing; written 2026-10-14)

**Idea.** Within each Korean trading session window, Z = 1 if at least one blind-coded de-escalation post (DEESC, after the 3-hour cluster filter; `posts_coded.json`) appeared in the window. The first stage (hourly data) showed WTI −1.3% in the 1–2 hours after such posts. Z is used as an instrument for the WTI change over the same window; the outcome is the Korean airline return in that window with chip controls. Exclusion restriction (not testable): de-escalation posts affect Korean airlines only through oil (they also reduce war risk generally, so the estimate is an upper bound on the pure fuel-price channel; the airline reaction to the war news itself is part of the reduced form).
**Windows.** Korean session d: from the previous Korean close (06:30 UTC of the previous Korean trading date) to the close of session d (06:30 UTC). WTI window return = 100·ln(CL close of the hourly bar ending at 06:30 UTC of d / close of the bar ending at 06:30 UTC of the previous trading date), computed from hourly bars; Korean returns from daily closes (Yahoo). Sample: Korean sessions from 2 March to 13 Oct 2026 (about 150) with complete WTI hourly bars.
**Variables.** Z_D = 1 if a DEESC post in the window; Z_E = 1 if an ESC post (second instrument, placebo-like: first stage expected near zero). Controls: previous U.S. session S&P 500 return, previous-session SOX return, same-session Korean chip factor (mean of Samsung and SK hynix; for the chip-outcome placebo the chip factor control is dropped). Outcomes: Korean Air, Jin Air, Jeju Air; placebo outcomes: chip factor itself, KOSPI.
**Models.** (1) First stage: ΔWTI = a + π Z_D + δ Z_E + controls; (2) reduced form: R = a + θ Z_D + γ Z_E + controls; (3) 2SLS of R on ΔWTI instrumented by Z_D (just-identified); Wald ratio θ/π; heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors (HC1); report first-stage F.
**Interpretation.** The estimate is credible only if the first stage is strong (F > 10) and the chip placebo shows no reduced-form effect. A negative 2SLS coefficient for Korean airlines means that oil down (de-escalation) lifts the airlines.
**Limitations.** Instrument is not randomly assigned and also carries war-risk information; many windows have both ESC and DEESC; around 40 treated windows; Korean daily returns include all other news in the window.
