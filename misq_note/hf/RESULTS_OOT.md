# Out-of-time test of the habituation model (pre-registration `THEORY_V3_HABITUATION.md`; `analysis_oot.py`; `results_oot.json`; `oot_signal_posts.csv`)
Posts 14 Feb 2022–13 Oct 2024 (not used to build the theory): 186 oil/geopolitics and 48 tariff keyword posts, each coded by two independent model instances (kappa: oil 0.62, tariffs 0.96). Daily outcomes (WTI; S&P 500), reaction on the trading day containing the post (after 16:00 ET → next trading day), standardized by the previous 20-day volatility. Primary labels = coders agree. Only **49 signal posts** remain (24 oil: 15 escalation, 9 de-escalation; 25 tariff: all escalation, no tariff de-escalation post in 2022–2024), on 42 distinct days; the rest are OTHER (domestic politics, 'would never have happened if I were president', bare links).
**H1 (primary, one-sided): not supported, and not informative.** Coefficient of n24 (same-topic signal posts in the previous 24 hours) on the standardized directional impact: **−0.09 (SE 0.29; one-sided p = 0.38; clustered by reaction day)**; log(1+hours since the last such post): −0.13 (SE 0.11; predicted positive; p = 0.12 for the opposite sign). Minimum detectable effect at 80% power: **0.82** standardized units for n24 — about **three times** the in-sample estimate it was meant to confirm (−0.24). The point estimate has the predicted sign, but the test cannot distinguish zero from −0.24 (95% CI about [−0.66, +0.48]).
**By topic:** oil n24 +0.09 (SE 0.24; MDE 0.67), log gap −0.21 (p = 0.13); tariffs n24 −0.68 (SE 0.70, MDE 1.97). **H2 (first-in-cluster dummy):** −0.25 (SE 0.33; predicted positive; p = 0.45). **H3 (ACTION × n24):** not estimable (ACTION posts are nearly absent in this period). **H4 placebo (OTHER posts' |z| on n24):** +0.17 (p = 0.29; n = 160), no relation. Leave-one-year-out estimates of n24: −0.11 (without 2022), −0.17 (without 2023), +0.81 (without 2024; p = 0.08): unstable.
**Decision under the pre-registered rule.** The out-of-time test **does not confirm** habituation (the primary coefficient is not significant), but it has too little power to refute it. The habituation result in the hourly data (all four topics pooled: −0.24, p = 0.036) therefore remains an exploratory, unreplicated finding.
**Required sample.** With the in-sample standard error (about 0.11 at 272 signal posts in hourly data), detecting −0.24 with 80% power needs about 480 signal posts with hourly data; in the 2022–2024 daily data, whose SE is 0.29 for 49 posts, about 560 posts would be required. The available 2022–2024 period has far too few relevant posts (a private citizen posting about tariffs and wars with a smaller market audience) and only daily returns. A confirmatory sample would have to come from prospective collection (new posts after 14 Oct 2026 with hourly or 5-minute data), expected to yield roughly 130–150 signal posts per year at the present rate, i.e., several years.
**Reading.** The theory-building sequence (binding constraint → salience → habituation) has not produced a confirmed general mechanism; each step was rejected or left inconclusive by the next sample, which the pre-registration documents. What remains established is descriptive: the Poisson–Gamma arrival of posts, the reliability of the LLM coding pipeline, and the large state-dependent response of oil, the dollar and Treasury futures to de-escalation posts during the Hormuz closure.
