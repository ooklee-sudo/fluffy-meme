# Extension of the intraday post-reaction study: replication, other oil-geopolitics posts, and a second coder (plan fixed before any new return is computed; written 2026-10-14)

**Starting point.** In the 2026 war sample (Amendment 1 of `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md`, exploratory), blind-coded de-escalation posts were followed by WTI −1.29% (n = 33, p < 0.001) while escalation posts had no effect (WTI −0.02%, p = 0.92). This plan tests whether the asymmetry **replicates out of sample** and **generalizes**, and whether it survives a **second, independent coder**.
**Hypotheses fixed now.** H_R1 (replication, Sample A): in Iran/Hormuz posts from 14 Oct 2024 to 27 Feb 2026, de-escalation posts (DEESC) are followed by negative WTI reaction (one-sided, mean < 0) and escalation posts (ESC) by no significant reaction. H_R2 (generalization, Sample B): for other oil-geopolitics posts (Russia/Ukraine, Venezuela, OPEC/Saudi/Iraq/Kuwait/Libya, oil/gasoline prices) with the same logic, de-escalation (sanctions relief, peace/deal, production increase) lowers WTI, escalation (new sanctions, strikes on energy infrastructure, blockade, threats to producers) does not raise it. H_R3 (coder robustness): results with coder 2 (an independent model instance) have the same signs, and Cohen's kappa between coders is reported (≥ 0.6 considered acceptable; this is not a substitute for human coding, which remains pending).
**Samples (selected by keyword before coding, not by returns).** Sample A: 65 posts matching Iran/Hormuz with a threat or peace term, 14 Oct 2024–28 Feb 2026. Sample B: 138 posts in the same window (and 34 in the 2026 war period) matching oil-geopolitics terms (`russia|ukrain|putin|zelensk|venezuela|maduro|opec|saudi|libya|iraq|kuwait|oil prices|oil|crude|gasoline|gas prices`) and a threat/peace/sanction/tariff/blockade term, not already in Sample A. Retweets without text excluded. Coding classes: ESC, DEESC, MIXED, OTHER (for Sample B, relative to oil supply/geopolitical risk of oil-producing countries; OTHER if the post is not about that). The 191 war-period Iran posts were already coded by coder 1 (the first model instance).
**Coders.** Coder 1 = first instance (existing for the 191; new instance for the 237 new posts). Coder 2 = an independent instance coding all 428 posts without seeing coder 1's output.
**Tests.** As in `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` Amendment 1: reaction = log change from the open of the hourly bar containing the post to the close of the next bar; 3-hour cluster filter; null = other bars of the same period with no coded post within ±2 h; 5,000 permutations; WTI, Brent, S&P 500, Nasdaq, SOX/NVDA (equities in regular hours only). Sample A requires hourly data back to Oct 2024 (Yahoo supports about 2 years).
**Pooling.** Pooled across samples (A + B + 2026 war), regress the WTI reaction on indicators DEESC and ESC with sample fixed effects (OLS, HC1), and test DEESC = −ESC? (asymmetry: DEESC coefficient differs from the negative of the ESC coefficient). Inference by permutation of the post labels within sample.
**Limitations.** Posts' timing is endogenous; the model coders are not human; hourly bars are coarse and futures roll; many posts overlap with news; multiple tests across WTI/Brent/equities (the primary outcome is WTI; others are secondary).
