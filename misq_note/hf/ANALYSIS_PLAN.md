# High-frequency reaction of oil futures and U.S. stocks to Trump's Iran posts: analysis plan (fixed before computing; written 2026-10-14)

**Why.** Daily closes mix many news items. Truth Social posts carry exact timestamps, and Yahoo Finance provides hourly bars of WTI (CL=F) and Brent (BZ=F) futures (nearly 24 h) and of U.S. equities (regular hours). This tests whether the posts themselves move oil and stocks in the hours after they appear, and provides a cleaner oil-price variation than daily closes. Disclosure: daily results for these posts are known (`../poisson_posts/RESULTS_MARKETS.md`: no weekly or daily association); no intraday return has been examined.
**Data.** Truth Social archive (CNN; created_at UTC), 1 March–13 Oct 2026; hourly bars (epoch timestamps, UTC) for CL=F, BZ=F, S&P 500, Nasdaq, SOX, NVDA, UAL, XOM, USD/KRW from Yahoo (about 1 year).
**Post types (fixed now).** N ('threat'): the S1 definition from `../poisson_posts/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` (Iran and a threat term). P ('peace'): a post mentioning Iran and one of: deal, ceasefire, cease-fire, agreement, peace, negotiat, talks, memorandum, reopen, \"open\" (case-insensitive) and not classified N. Re-posts without text are excluded. Within a cluster, only the first post is kept: a post is dropped if another N/P post occurred in the previous 3 hours.
**Window.** For a post at time t, let k be the hourly bar containing t; reaction R = 100·ln(close of bar k+1 / open of bar k), a window of 1–2 hours after the post plus the portion of the hour before it (a conservative, noisy measure because the exact post time inside the bar is not used). Bars must be consecutive (bar k+1 starts one hour after bar k).
**Null distribution.** The same two-hour return over every bar k since 1 March 2026 that has no N/P post within the 2 hours before the bar start through the 2 hours after, with bars classified by whether they are within regular U.S. equity hours.
**Tests.** (1) Oil: mean R for N posts versus the null (expected positive), P posts (expected negative), two-sided permutation p (5,000), also the magnitude |R|. (2) Equities (S&P 500, Nasdaq, SOX, NVDA, UAL; regular hours only): N expected negative, P expected positive. (3) Instrument check: first-stage strength = the standard deviation and t-statistic of R_oil on N/P posts. (4) If the first stage is meaningful, a Wald ratio: ΔKorean-Air-next-day / ΔWTI on post days (exploratory).
**Limitations.** Posts are not randomly timed (they respond to events and to the market); the hour bar blurs the exact timing; other news in the same hour; classification by keywords; Brent/WTI futures roll (continuous front-month series); the 1-year data cover all months but 5-minute data only the last 60 days.
