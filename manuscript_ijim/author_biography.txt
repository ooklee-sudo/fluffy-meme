# Author biography

CENTER: **Ook Lee**

Ook Lee is a Professor in the Department of Information System at Hanyang University in Seoul, Korea. He received a Ph.D. in Management of Information Systems from Claremont Graduate University, USA. He was a faculty member at the University of Queensland, Australia; the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, USA; and North Carolina A&T State University, USA. His research interests are artificial intelligence, software maintenance, information systems economics, and technical information systems.
