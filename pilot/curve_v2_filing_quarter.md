# LLM-drafted-like share by filing quarter (prevalence_curve.py, 15 weekly samples pooled; run on the user's computer)

Data: `filing_quarter_data.csv`; figure: `figures/prevalence_by_filing_quarter.png` (script `plot_filing.py`).

Document-weighted mean alpha-hat by filing period: 2019Q1–2022Q3: 0.020 (n=14,811); 2022Q4–2023Q1: 0.009 (n=2,188); 2023Q2–2024Q2: 0.033 (n=5,467); 2024Q3–2025Q1: 0.040 (n=2,774); 2025Q2–2026Q2: 0.055 (n=4,110).
Reading: filings up to 2022Q3 are flat (calibrated share ≈0–0.9%); filings of 2022Q4–2023Q1 are at or below baseline; a clear break appears with filings of 2023Q2 (alpha-hat 0.029, calibrated 1.2%), followed by a rise of roughly one percentage point per half year to about 5% (calibrated) by 2025Q4 (alpha-hat 0.064, calibrated 5.4%). The two latest quarters rest on few, early-published documents (2026Q1: 292 docs, alpha-hat 0.086 with a wide interval; 2026Q2: 622 docs, 0.049) and should not be over-read.
