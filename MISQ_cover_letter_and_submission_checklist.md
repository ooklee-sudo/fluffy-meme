## A. 커버레터 초안 (영문, 대괄호는 직접 채우기)

Dear Editors of *MIS Quarterly*,

We submit "Administered Ethics: How Data-Protection Enforcement and AI Legislation Select Procedural Harm in a Developmental State" for consideration as [article type per MISQ guidelines].

IS research increasingly treats regulation as the means of reconciling the ethical risks of AI with its promise, but it rarely asks how regulatory instruments decide what counts as an ethical harm. Drawing on critical social theory, we develop the concept of administered ethics and a set of mechanisms and falsifiable propositions. We examine it through a critical documentary study of South Korea, where an independent privacy regulator, early AI-specific enforcement, and a comprehensive AI statute make the thesis less likely to hold. Three core records are analyzed in depth, and an extended corpus of regulator decisions and authorization instruments tests recurrence and searches for disconfirming instances.

We believe the paper is a fit for MISQ for three reasons. First, it extends IS research on regulation of and through technology by asking whose conflicts a rule–artifact coupling can express, not only whether the coupling succeeds. Second, it offers a portable, methodologically explicit analytic procedure for critical IS research. Third, it states scope conditions under which the account should transfer to other regimes and conditions under which it would be falsified.
