# Suggested reviewers for the Information & Management submission

Enter these in the Editorial Manager form, not in the cover letter. Email addresses are not listed
here; take them from each person's institutional profile. Before submitting, confirm that none of them
has co-authored with you, shared your institution, or worked closely with you in the past few years.

## Recommended (5)

| Name | Affiliation | Expertise relevant to the paper | Reason for suggestion (paste into the form) | Profile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aaron Baird | Georgia State University, Computer Information Systems | Delegation to agentic IS artifacts (Baird & Maruping, 2021, MISQ) | The paper extends the theory of delegation to agentic IS artifacts by showing that an agent's decision quality is state dependent. | https://aisel.aisnet.org/misq/vol45/iss1/12 |
| Andreas Fügener | University of Cologne | Experiments on human-AI collaboration and delegation (ISR, 2022) | Expert in experimental studies of delegation between humans and AI, the setting the paper's implications address. | https://wiso.uni-koeln.de/en/research/find-an-expert/experts/prof-dr-andreas-fuegener |

## Not suggested, and why

- Kieran Conboy (editor-in-chief, European Journal of Information Systems) and Patrick Mikalef
  (senior editor, EJIS): an earlier, theory-only version of this work was desk-rejected at EJIS.
  Their papers are cited and relevant, but suggesting them could look like steering toward or away
  from a prior decision.
- Patrick Mikalef also serves as an editor of Information & Management, which is a further reason
  not to suggest him.
- Current I&M editors: the journal assigns its own editors; check the editorial board page on
  ScienceDirect and remove anyone listed there from the suggestions above.
