## Regular-article version: what changed and what to watch
- The Replication Corner versions are kept in `docs/` (manuscript_replication_corner.md, web_appendix_replication_corner.md) in the repository, in case you resubmit there.
- New: Table 4 and Section 4.4 (direction of the disagreement and rating level), Web Appendix G (Tables G1, G2), a post hoc alternative hypothesis (Section 2.3: critical text as informative; Cao et al. 2011, Yin et al. 2014), and a disclosure of earlier unreported exploratory content-feature analyses (Process paragraph). These analyses were done after the main results were seen; the manuscript labels them exploratory. The ratio test of the two directions has p = .036 (our prompt) and .007 (original prompt) with no multiplicity correction.
- A regular article is judged on contribution, not only on replication; reviewers may find the theoretical contribution thin. The manuscript frames the contribution as a failed replication of the sign plus a qualified interpretation of the gap.
- Verify the new references (Cao et al. 2011; Yin et al. 2014) in the databases you use and that the sentences citing them match the papers.
- Check whether the journal counts appendix tables toward the table limit (main text: 4 tables; online appendix: 4 tables: C, E1, G1, G2).
