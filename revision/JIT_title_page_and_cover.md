## Cover letter statement (80 words maximum)

We show that occupation-level exposure designs, the main evidence on generative AI and jobs, cannot detect substitution of the size seen on freelance platforms, and that reported use overstates task reassignment. We extend IS assimilation theory by distinguishing judgment from production complements and treating assimilation as task-level, then specify a firm-level test. The argument applies wherever generated output must be judged, fitting the Journal's interest in theoretically grounded IS research relevant across contexts.
